## Projects

### 1. Online Retail Revenue and Customer Insights (SQL)
Which markets, products and customer segments drive or risk revenue in a UK e-commerce dataset. Uses joins, aggregation, filtering and CASE logic, and flags a data quality issue before drawing conclusions.

- The UK generated £8.2M in revenue, over 28x the next largest market (Netherlands, £285K).
- The Regency Cakestand was the top product at £165K.
- Also identifies an at-risk customer segment and a seasonal demand pattern.

[Read the full project](SQL_Portfolio_Project_Online_Retail.md)

### 2. Employee Attrition Analysis (SQL)
Which factors are most associated with employees leaving, using the IBM HR Analytics dataset (1,470 records). Uses filtering, multi-level grouping and CASE statements.

- Employees working overtime leave at 30.5%, against 10.4% for those who do not.
- Attrition is higher in Sales (20.6%) and HR (19.0%) than in R&D (13.8%).
- Attrition falls as job satisfaction rises, from 22.8% to 11.3%.

[Read the full project](SQL_Portfolio_Project_HR_Attrition.md)
